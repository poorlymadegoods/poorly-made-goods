Honest

Poorly Made Goods was born from a simple idea: stop pretending. Most leather brands want you to believe their wallets were forged in a Florentine dungeon by a 90-year-old artisan. Ours are made by us, in Chicago, with a decent number of YouTube tutorials.

We use real full-grain leather, real thread, and real skill. No excuses. No fluff. No origin stories. Just quality goods made by real people who are still figuring it out — and proud of it.
